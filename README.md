# 🌱 Jan Jagruk — Smart Waste Management Platform

### Awareness Meets Action ♻️

Jan Jagruk is an AI-powered smart waste management and citizen engagement platform designed to connect citizens, administrators, and waste-management teams through a single digital platform.

The platform enables citizens to report waste-related issues, track complaints, request pickups, and participate in waste segregation activities, while administrators can monitor waste incidents, identify hotspots, manage pickups, and track community participation.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Urban waste management often faces challenges such as:

- Overflowing garbage bins
- Garbage dumped on roads and public spaces
- Missed waste collection
- Illegal dumping
- Delayed complaint resolution
- Lack of centralized waste-management data
- Low citizen participation in proper waste segregation

Jan Jagruk aims to bridge the gap between citizens and waste-management authorities through technology, location intelligence, and AI-assisted features.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Jan Jagruk provides a centralized platform where citizens can report waste problems and authorities can efficiently manage, prioritize, and resolve them.

### 🔥 Core Innovation — 10-Report Smart Incident Detection

Instead of treating every complaint as a completely separate issue, Jan Jagruk can identify multiple related reports from the same area.

When multiple reports are detected within a defined geographic and time range, they can be grouped into a single waste incident.

### Example

Citizen 1 → Reports garbage  
Citizen 2 → Reports garbage  
Citizen 3 → Reports garbage  
...  
Citizen 10 → Reports garbage
